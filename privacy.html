Privacy policy

Last updated October 9, 2026.

Money is a personal app used only by its owner. Nobody else can sign in to it.

What it reads

With the owner's permission, Money reads the owner's Gmail with read-only access. It looks for bank, card and receipt emails and pulls out the date, amount, account and merchant. It never sends, deletes or changes email.

Where the data goes

  • Everything it reads is stored on the owner's own computer. There is no Money server in the cloud.
  • When an email is not in a known format, its text may be sent to Anthropic's Claude API to read the amount and merchant. Anthropic does not use API data to train its models.
  • Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or given to anyone else.

Google user data

Money's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove Money's access at any time from their Google Account under Security, then Third-party apps and services.

Contact

Questions go to the owner through this project's issue page.
